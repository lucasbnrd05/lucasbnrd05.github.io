What's inside

Wofi is used in two ways: as an app launcher (Super + D) and as a dmenu replacement for my Waybar menus (wifi, bluetooth, sysinfo, mode switcher). The style uses the Gruvbox red accent (#cc241d) to match the Hyprland theme.
