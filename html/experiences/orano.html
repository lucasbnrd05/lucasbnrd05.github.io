Overview

IT & DevOps Support Officer at Orano Support, within the IT & DevOps department. A 5-month mission from September 2026 to January 2027, in a critical industrial environment with strong security, confidentiality and business-continuity constraints.

Context

Orano is a French multinational specialized in the nuclear fuel cycle. Orano Support provides cross-functional IT and industrial support to the group's entities. Working in this environment means dealing with strict security requirements, high availability constraints, and rigorous documentation processes — a completely different scale from academic or startup projects.

Missions

Development Tools Support

  • Support on internal development tools, mainly around Python and GitLab.
  • Assistance to development teams on their working environments.
  • Participation in the use and administration of the artifact repository Nexus.

DevOps Practices

  • Contribution to activities related to CI/CD pipelines.
  • Support for improving DevOps practices and the associated documentation.
  • Coordination of technical information between teams and services.

Cybersecurity Support

  • Tracking the integration of security requirements within projects.
  • Support on security reviews and good practices.
  • Raising awareness on secure development within the teams.

Documentation & Business Continuity

  • Contribution to the update of the technical documentation.
  • Participation in works related to PCA / PRA (Business Continuity Plan / Disaster Recovery Plan) — a critical requirement in a nuclear environment.
  • Update and distribution of application reference documentation.

Team & Coordination

  • Participation in team meetings and internal exchanges.
  • Contribution to ongoing projects depending on needs.
  • Support to information coordination between technical teams.

What I Learned

Technical

  • Working with a real enterprise CI/CD workflow (not just academic builds).
  • Managing artifacts and dependencies at scale via Nexus.
  • Understanding how security requirements are integrated into the SDLC.
  • Documentation as a deliverable — not an afterthought.

Soft Skills

  • Communication: adapting technical explanations for different audiences (developers, security, management).
  • Coordination: working across teams with conflicting priorities.
  • Rigor: understanding why documentation and traceability matter in a critical industry.
  • Autonomy: supporting developers without blocking their work.

Why This Experience Matters

Working at Orano Support is not just "an internship in IT". It is a first immersion into an environment where a mistake can have real consequences. It completely reshaped how I think about software engineering: security, traceability, documentation and continuity are not optional — they are the foundation of every decision.

Lucas Bernardeau
